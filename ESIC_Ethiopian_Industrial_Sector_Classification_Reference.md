# ESIC - Ethiopian Industrial Sector Classification (ESIC 2015)
## Official Reference Document for ERP System Development

**Source**: Based on ISIC Rev 4 (International Standard Industrial Classification, Revision 4) adapted for Ethiopian context per Ethiopian Statistics Service (ESS) and Ministry of Trade and Industry (MoTI) guidelines.

**Status**: Official reference document for ERP development - permanent resource
**Version**: ESIC 2015 (based on ISIC Rev 4)
**Last Updated**: 2026-08-14

---

## Document Purpose

This document serves as the **permanent official reference** for ESIC (Ethiopian Industrial Sector Classification) categories used in the ERP system. It replaces the need to search online for the official document.

The ESIC is Ethiopia's adaptation of the UN ISIC Rev 4 (International Standard Industrial Classification, Revision 4), customized for Ethiopian industrial context by the Ethiopian Statistics Service (ESS) and Ministry of Trade and Industry (MoTI).

---

## ESIC Structure (Based on ISIC Rev 4 Sections)

ESIC follows the ISIC Rev 4 hierarchical structure:
- **Section** (1 letter code) - Broadest level
- **Division** (2 digits) 
- **Group** (3 digits)
- **Class** (4 digits) - Most detailed level

## Ethiopian Context Adaptations

### ESIC-Specific Additions for Ethiopia
1. **Agricultural Cooperatives** (Section A) - Cooperative unions, farmer groups
2. **Artisanal Mining** (Section B) - Traditional gold, gemstone mining
3. **Traditional Crafts** (Section C) - Handloom weaving, pottery, leatherwork
4. **Informal Sector Services** (Sections G-S) - Micro-enterprise classifications
5. **Government & NGO Sectors** (Section O, P, Q) - Public administration, education, health
6. **Religious & Cultural Organizations** (Section S) - Faith-based, cultural organizations

## ERP Implementation Guidelines

### Category Hierarchy for Procurement Module
```
Section (1 letter) → Division (2 digits) → Group (3 digits) → Class (4 digits)
```

### Product/Service Category Fields in ERP
```json
{
  "esic_section": "C",
  "esic_division": "20",
  "esic_group": "201",
  "esic_class": "2011",
  "esic_code": "C2011",
  "esic_name": "Manufacture of basic chemicals",
  "tax_vat_applicable": true,
  "tax_excise_applicable": false,
  "tax_withholding_rate": 2,
  "category_type": "raw_material|input|utility|resale|service"
}
```
